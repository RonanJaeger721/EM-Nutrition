Chronic condition management

Core goals

HIV nutrition support

Nutrition care can support immune function, healthy weight maintenance, medication tolerance, and long-term risk reduction.

  • Support immune function with consistent, nutrient-dense meals.
  • Maintain a healthy weight while helping prevent wasting or unwanted weight gain.
  • Manage medication-related side effects that affect appetite, digestion, or intake.
  • Reduce the risk of related conditions such as diabetes and heart disease.

Core principles

Cancer support

Nutrition support during and after cancer treatment focuses on strength, tolerance, hydration, and practical food choices during changing symptoms.

  • Prioritize protein to support repair, muscle maintenance, and recovery.
  • Protect calorie intake when appetite is low or needs are higher.
  • Stay hydrated with realistic fluid strategies.
  • Adjust meals around common side effects such as nausea, taste changes, mouth discomfort, or fatigue.

Key habits

Diabetes nutrition care

Diabetes support is built around steady routines, balanced meals, and learning how the body responds to food, medication, and activity.

  • Create regular meal patterns that help reduce glucose spikes and crashes.
  • Monitor blood glucose responses to meals and snacks where appropriate.
  • Adjust food timing and portions around medication, movement, and daily schedule.
  • Build sustainable carbohydrate, fibre, protein, and hydration habits.
WhatsApp about diabetes Email EM Nutrition
Key principles

Renal failure nutrition care

Renal nutrition must be highly individualized, guided by kidney function, dialysis status, lab trends, fluid needs, and other conditions.

  • Adjust protein needs for non-dialysis CKD, dialysis, or AKI.
  • Manage sodium and fluid intake according to clinical needs.
  • Monitor potassium and phosphorus intake based on blood results.
  • Support energy needs with appropriate calories and carbohydrate planning.

Care is tailored around eGFR, dialysis modality, lab trends, symptoms, and comorbidities.

WhatsApp about renal care Email EM Nutrition
The goal

GERD nutrition support

The goal is to reduce reflux episodes, minimize esophageal irritation, and limit triggers that can relax the lower esophageal sphincter.

  • Identify personal food and drink triggers without unnecessary restriction.
  • Support meal timing, portions, and posture habits that reduce reflux pressure.
  • Use weight, diet, and lifestyle strategies where they are relevant.
  • Create a plan that fits daily routines and reduces flare-ups over time.
WhatsApp about GERD Email EM Nutrition
Core goal

PMOS nutrition support

PMOS support focuses on improving insulin sensitivity, supporting hormone balance, and reducing chronic inflammation, not only weight loss.

  • Build balanced meals that support steady energy and glucose response.
  • Support lower inflammation through practical food quality changes.
  • Use nutrition and lifestyle habits that support androgen and metabolic health.
  • Create a plan that respects symptoms, goals, routine, and body confidence.
WhatsApp about PMOS Email EM Nutrition
Management focus

Hypertension nutrition care

Nutrition support for chronic hypertension focuses on diet and lifestyle adjustments that help protect heart and vascular health.

  • Improve sodium awareness and build flavourful lower-sodium routines.
  • Support potassium-rich choices where clinically appropriate.
  • Encourage heart-supportive meal patterns, hydration, and movement habits.
  • Align changes with medication, blood pressure monitoring, and personal goals.
